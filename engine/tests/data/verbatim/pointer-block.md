## Vesta

This project keeps its settled decisions under `vesta/`. `vesta/INTERNALS.md` explains the mechanism.

- At the start of a task, run `/vesta-hook` with the task. It loads the decisions that bind the work.
- When the owner corrects or redirects you, do what `/vesta-hook` said about noting it: who notes a
  correction differs by machine, and its sheet says which holds here.
- Before reporting a task as done, run `/vesta-insight`.
- Change nothing under `vesta/` by hand. Lasting changes, settings included, are drafted as proposals and
  applied only when the owner approves them.
- When telling the owner anything about Vesta, use plain words: decision, note, proposal.
