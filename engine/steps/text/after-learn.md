and from how many sessions; how many groups there were and how many notes fell in none; each proposal
with what became of it; each note kept open, by its title, and what it waits for; whether the
conditions for revisiting decisions were gone through and what that found; what was done about
decisions that say the same thing; and what the pass did not do.

Use plain words with the owner: decision, note, proposal, approve, reject. Name a record by its label
and title, and a note by its title. Leave out
the sheet's own words: walk, row, lens, latch, bar, ladder, sweep, mark, bundle,
trigger, index, window, held; and never give an internal id.
