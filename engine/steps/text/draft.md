For each routed group, run `propose <kind> --target <target> --evidence <ids> --source {pass}` and
fill the proposal: the shape at its general level, the counter-case, the cheaper homes considered,
the change. A proposed record states the general constraint, never a list of the instances.

Then put five questions to each draft, and correct what they find:

- Is it true?
- Would its {when} line be recognized by someone starting the task it should catch?
- Does an existing record already cover it? `proposals <id>` prints the records that may.
- Is it stated at the level of the shape?
- Does every claim have an anchor?

`proposals <id>` also says what still stops the proposal from being applied; leave none of that
standing. Where no group was routed, draft nothing.
