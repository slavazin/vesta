# Start walk

Walked by `/vesta-hook` at the start of a task, together with the steps the engine supplies. The rows and
lenses here are this project's own; each was added, or will be changed, through an approved proposal. The
`level` column is the lowest engagement level at which a row or lens is used.

## Rows

| id | fires-when | fires-on | consult | owed act | level | origin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| start-01 | the task rests on how something outside the project behaves — a tool, a service, a standard, a reference work | — | that thing's own documentation or text | check the claim at the source before building on it | standard | seed |

## Lenses

| lens | angle | counterfactual | level | origin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| said-before | Which part of this task has the owner asked for, corrected, or explained in an earlier session, and where is the earlier answer written down? | Is it written down where this session would have found it without being told, or would the owner have had to say it again? | standard | seed |
| two-readings | Where could this task be read two ways, such that the two readings would produce different work? | Would either reading do, or would the owner mind which was taken? Ask only where they would mind. | active | seed |
| done-means | What will the owner look at to decide this task is done, and does the request say so? | Is that already plain from the request or from a loaded decision? | active | seed |
